# Aegle Therapeutics

> Aegle Therapeutics Corporation is a privately held, clinical-stage regenerative medicine
> company developing extracellular vesicle (EV) therapy — a cell-free therapeutic composite
> of EVs, including exosomes, isolated from allogeneic bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem
> cells. Its lead candidate, AGLE-102, is in a Phase 1/2 clinical study for Recessive
> Dystrophic Epidermolysis Bullosa (RDEB).
